# Licensing Outreach Templates (DigitAlb / Top Channel)

Use these templates to request official distribution rights and technical feed access for your IPTV platform.

My name is [Your Name], and I am [Role] at [Company Name], an IPTV service operating in [Countries/Regions].

- [DigitAlb package / Top Channel / Big Brother VIP channels]

- Distribution rights details (territory, duration, content scope)
- Commercial model (wholesale/reseller/minimum guarantees/revenue share)
- Technical onboarding requirements
- API/feed delivery options (HLS/DASH, tokenized URLs, DRM, concurrency limits)
- EPG/logo metadata options
- SLA and support contacts for incident handling

Technical profile of our platform:
- Devices: [Android TV, mobile, web, smart TV]
- Delivery: [M3U/Xtream/API integration]
- DRM/token support: [Yes/No]
- Expected active users: [number]
- Target launch window: [date]

Please share your partner onboarding process and the best contact for commercial and technical kickoff.

Thank you,
